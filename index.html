About the Institute

Burdwan Youth Computer Training Centre (BYCTC) is a computer-training institution serving learners in Burdwan with a practical, career-oriented approach to digital education. The centre focuses on usable computer skills—from office applications to accounting, GST, artificial intelligence and emerging digital skills.
